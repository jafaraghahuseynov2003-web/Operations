Operations analytics.
Overview

This repository contains a collection of projects focused on Operations Analytics, Process Improvement and Operational Monitoring.

The aim is to apply data and analysis techniques for finding issues in operations, evaluating the efficiency of processes, identifying potential threats, making decisions based on evidence.

Projects:
01 - Order Operations.

Analyze order operational performance (processing time, delay/cancellation rate, SLA).

Focus topics:

Time to process order;

Delivery performance;

Bottlenecking and delays;

Cancellation analysis

Operational KPI's.

02 - Incident Analysis.

Review operations incident to find trends, root cause and opportunities for process enhancements.

Focus areas:

Incidence rate;

Types of incidents;

Root cause analysis;

Repeated issues in operations;

Impact study.

03 - Risk Assessment.

Analytical study of the risk in operations, creation of models to detect risky situations.

Focuses:

Risk recognition;

Risk scoring;

Past Risk Trends;

Predictive analytics;

Monitoring risks.

04 - Process monitoring.

Development of methods for monitoring the operation process and identifying anomalies.

Focus areas:

Monitoring KPIs;

Thresholds & Alerts;

Process deviations;

Monitoring of service level.

Operating control.

05 – Automation.

Automate repetitive operational data processing & reporting activities.

Focus areas:

Data processing;

Automated reporting;

Automated repetitive tasks;

Data validation;

Reporting work flows.

06 - Operational Dashboard.

Creation of dashboards to track operation metrics and assist in managerial decision making.

Focus areas:

Operational KPIs;

Performance trends;

Bottleneck detection;

Reporting interactif;

Visibility management

Projects generally follow the following workflow:

Data collection and preparation

Data quality checks

Exploratory data analysis

KPI definition and calculation
